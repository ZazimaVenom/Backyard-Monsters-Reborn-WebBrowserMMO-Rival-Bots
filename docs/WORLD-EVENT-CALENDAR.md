# World event calendar

Open **Yard events → World event calendar** from home. The start and end dates use UTC for everyone on the server. Enter an open round, play its waves or raids from the calendar and open **Standings** to see completed scores. The table shows the leading 50 keepers and your personal score. The event standings and the invasion ranking have separate scores.

| Event | Start | Competition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creature Carnage | Every Monday | Six days of raids, then one day until the next round |
| Monster Blitzkrieg | Every Monday | Six days of home defenses, then one day until the next round |
| Wild Monster Invasion I | January, May and September 10 | Seven days |
| Wild Monster Invasion II | March, July and November 10 | Seven days |
| Brukkarg War | The 24th of January, March, May, July, September and November | Five days, followed by two days of final standings |

Entry requires the event's usual yard facilities and Town Hall progression. A private campaign and a competition keep independent stages and deadlines. **Return to private progress** restores the private campaign. Re-entering an active competition resumes that round. The home yard, destroyed structures, actual troops and permanently earned items belong to the keeper across both modes. Finish the current invasion or defense before switching runs. Only server-confirmed progress earned during the round counts toward its standings.

The weekly and four-month schedules follow the recurrence specifications in the supplied server's EventCalendarConfig and the client event classes. The calendar continues across years. It also works alongside the private on-demand versions for keepers who missed a round.
