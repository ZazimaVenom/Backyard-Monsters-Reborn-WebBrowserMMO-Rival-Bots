# Chaos Factory, Chaos Lab and siege device artwork

Each facility and device received its own generated HD illustration. The source game's colored facility bodies and animated frames supplied the pose, palette and silhouette references. All five shipping images retain their generated dimensions and transparency. The central pane of the Candy Jar is translucent so the Canvas renderer can show the trapped tower through the glass.

The colored device references were extracted at the frame dimensions defined in `SPRITES.as` and `com/monsters/siege/weapons/Jars.as`. The loot device references its hose and head together. The original game lists the device names in `server/public/gamestage/assets/english.json`. All shipped illustrations are new renders from the built-in image generator. Sizes and SHA-256 hashes appear in the art manifest.
