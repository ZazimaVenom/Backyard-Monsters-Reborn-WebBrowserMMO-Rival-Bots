# Yard events

Open **Yard events** at home or select the object in your yard. Other keepers can see the objects when they visit. Only the owner can send workers, open the gift or release the horse. These events belong to the human keeper's surface home. Bot economics and online-only invasion scheduling run independently.

## Halloween

Halloween starts October 30 at 00:00 UTC and runs until November 2 at 00:00 UTC. Outside those dates, a keeper can open a three-day local replay once per UTC calendar year. The replay shares its annual goodie bag with that year's Halloween. Reloading, revisiting or switching between the two windows preserves claims.

The patch tops up to four or five ordinary pumpkins the first time the owner visits each UTC day. Their positions persist until they are picked or reach 24 hours old. Sending a worker takes two seconds. It occupies a real construction worker. Each pumpkin has one hidden result, and reloading while it shakes resumes the same job. Half the pumpkins release a harmless twig bomb at yard center. The other half deliver one of the four resources, normally 2–4% of storage capacity, with a one-in-four chance of 7–12%. Delivery is limited to available storage space.

Open the giant pumpkin to receive 100 Shiny, one D.A.V.E. Pumpkin and six Mini-Pumpkin decorations. The gift grants an ornament type only if you have none of that type on the map or in storage. New decorations go to storage. A second request cannot duplicate the reward.

## Trojan Horse

Horsey appears once at the northern edge of a human home with experience level nine or higher and over 2,000,000 points. The release uses the source building-cost and timing tables, the original one-tenth yard valuation and one-third prior-tier upgrade credit. It also counts half the resources collected from your producers and net surface home raid loot earned under this release. Previously unrecorded historical loot, gifts, fortifications and paid offers are not reconstructed. The release keeps the highest observed structural value after event progress begins. The original level table is separate from your Town Hall and league rank.

Opening the horse starts a real home defense. Fifty-one monsters enter from the north in the source species order, skipping Eye-ra. The original frame-1 and 20-frame arrivals are projected at 30 frames per second onto the combat engine's quarter-second ticks. Their HP and damage scale to 40%, 60%, 80% or 100% based on the 2, 3, 5 and 8 million point thresholds. You can reload and resume, or sound the siren. Buildings keep the damage they took in combat. The free **Repair Base** button restores them. There is one horse encounter per keeper and no ranked reward.

The source references are WMATTACK.as, CUSTOMATTACKS.as, BUILDING27.as, BASE.as, BFOUNDATION.as, BRESOURCE.as, HalloweenConfig.ts and the classes under com/monsters/halloween. The locally playable replay and 32-tile coordinates adapt the original events to this server.
