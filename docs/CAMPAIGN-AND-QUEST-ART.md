# Wild tribe scouting and quest journal artwork

The campaign scouting screen uses an original illustration of a river valley and a timber-and-scrap encampment. The quest journal features an original teal quartermaster with a field book. Both pieces were commissioned individually from the built-in image generator for this remake. The camp is an atmospheric scouting illustration. Individual enemy layouts appear through the game's actual yard renderer.

Each production WebP keeps the generated pixel dimensions. The portrait retains its full original alpha channel. Headings, quest names, rewards and controls are rendered in HTML. File sizes, dimensions and SHA-256 digests are recorded in the art manifest.

## Scouting valley generation prompt

Use case: stylized concept art. Produce a beautiful finished WIDE landscape environment illustration for the scouting expeditions panel of a polished Backyard Monsters browser-game remake. Paint a playable-looking miniature wilderness: approach over a mossy rock ledge from upper-left sunlight, across a sunlit amber-earth trail that snakes from the lower middle into a fortified wild monster encampment on the middle-right third. Camp identity: salvaged crooked timber and blue-grey metal palisades, hand-tied copper bands, two deep burnt-orange tribal pennants with abstract claw slashes but absolutely NO lettering, a circular sheltered monster den with golden fireglow, a little defensive lookout, details draw the eye to camp. Lush jade mature trees, warm grassy clearings, hints of cobalt river and distant blue ridges separate foreground, middle ground and background. Capture the colorful smooth ink-and-paint HD game look of Digimon Dawn/Dusk field artwork upgraded for an HD monitor: sculpted shapes, precise controlled dark contours, natural paint gradients, strong readable silhouettes, matte soft foliage, vibrant saturated greens and copper highlights. No visible people, no text, no logos, no buttons, no map pins or board grid, no borders. Cinematic wide 2.7:1 scene composition, most important landmark in the middle-right at x=65% and y=52%, broadly readable even when cropped shallow to banner height; quiet dark semi-shaded tree branches on the far left provide breathing space for an overlay. Frame the hero camp unobstructed. Supply one finished edge-to-edge landscape painting, opaque.
 
## Quest quartermaster generation prompt

Use case: stylized concept. One original NPC character portrait, isolated on a TRUE transparent alpha background, for a professional HD monster game quest journal. Design the yard quartermaster as a small, exceptionally expressive amphibian monster: rich turquoise sea-green rounded body, lighter mint throat, a broad friendly salamander face, two subtle swept-back fin ears edged cobalt, large amber eyes with a little spark and dark inky rim; short, folded cinnamon scarf at neck; a weathered brown crossbody strap and ONE treasured palm-sized field journal clasped against its chest with both stubby three-fingered hands. The book has a warm ochre cover, red stitch binding, a little blank brass corner plate and green ribbon bookmark, with no writing. This is an intelligent yard denizen greeting its keeper with a friendly confident smile, facing towards the viewer's left, compact toad-like proportions and a coherent simple silhouette. Frame the single figure as a tight waist-up portrait, eye-line about upper third, with all fins, head and book safely inside the square. Hint of engraved leather grain and skin highlights, graphic readable anatomy. Match a smooth richly painted HD reimagining of Digimon Dusk/Dawn monster sprites, clean sculptural cel shading, subtle texture on materials, crisp tapered deep-colored outlines, upper-left sunlight, visually generous bright highlights. Strong silhouette at 64 pixels; restrained warm and cool contrast with teal, emerald, orange and brass. Pure transparency around the whole portrait; no floor, no scenery, no sticker outline, no cast shadow, no lettering, no logo, no companion, no additional character states, no UI.
