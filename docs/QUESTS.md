# Quest journal

Open **Quests** in the sidebar. **Getting started** walks a new keeper through the Town Hall, resource buildings, Hatchery, Housing, Flinger and first three hatchlings. **Rewards ready** collects every payable objective in one place. Choose **Collect** on a single award to put its resources into storage or its monsters into your army. The **Achievements** section records badges automatically.

## What counts

The 0.13 journal has 148 entries. It includes 84 overworld quests from the original client, all 38 Inferno quests, five local quests and 21 achievement badges. Construction, research and hatching count on completion. Existing finished structures, unlocks, champion levels, holdings, research and ranked victories are recognized. Queued eggs, tutorial starting troops and gifted monsters do not count as newly hatched. Successful resource collections, juicing and confirmed enemy casualties are tracked from this release onward. The server cannot reconstruct undocumented earlier collections and casualties.

Each keeper has a private, permanent ledger. Confirmed camp Town Hall destruction counts toward the four tribe objectives. Completing a camp through general destruction alone leaves its Hall objective open. A real home defense can earn First Blood. Practice battles do not credit kills. Wins on the league table continue to use only ranked battles. Visiting another keeper never exposes their journal.

Awards fit as a whole. If a resource store or Monster Housing lacks space, the journal shows how much more is needed. Queued eggs reserve housing too. A claim never discards overflow, increases a capacity, unlocks a rewarded monster for future hatching or changes rank. Duplicate requests return the original claimed state. Claims wait for the keeper's active invasion to finish.

## Source and local tuning

The reference is the supplied Refitted client, primarily `client/scripts/QUESTS.as` (overworld definitions, persistent completion flags and collection), `QUESTSPOPUP.as` (group list), `ACHIEVEMENTS.as` (badges), `BRESOURCE.as` (largest single harvest), `BUILDING9.as` (juicing), `ChampionBase.as` (evolution 6), `WMBASE.as` (tribe Hall victories), and the English language catalog.

- The journal preserves original titles, quest IDs, ordinary/champion identities, resource families, and creature reward counts. It includes the building and producer chains, twelve Locker quests UC2–UC13, four harvests, four Juicer goals, all six basic champion quests, First Blood, four tribe Town Halls, nine Chaos device research quests, and six mushroom-picking quests. The Foraging section tracks ordinary and golden mushrooms harvested by your own workers. Device rewards fit into the single Chaos Factory slot and require an operational factory. Unlock rewards require a completed Locker, including for species the remake grants at creation. The two original tutorial-only goals C0 and C1 become visible onboarding entries. Independently satisfied goals can be collected in any order.
- Resource awards and single-click harvest thresholds use one tenth of the source amounts. The home **Collect resources** action totals the actual amount credited across all bins. The Stockpile threshold is 250,000 of each resource at once. Badge A6 uses a completed level-two local Map Room. First Blood credits settled enemy creature casualties in real invasions.
- The five local goals cover newly hatched monsters, an Academy improvement, a Lab ability, a ranked win and a conquered holding. The 21 source-derived badges have local display names and no payouts. The only omitted quest ID in the supplied 85-entry overworld table is FAN, which depended on Facebook verifying a page like.

## Cavern, neighbors and outposts

Open **Cavern quests** from your underground yard, or select the three **Cavern** sections in the main journal. The 38 entries in `client/scripts/INFERNO_QUESTS.as` supply 27 building objectives, seven discoveries and four single-collection objectives. Existing completed facilities, discoveries and the 13 Moloch Descent maps count on upgrade. Resource awards use the local one-tenth scale and go into the cavern bank. Troop awards retain the original species and quantity, require free Compound space and join the underground army. Queued eggs reserve Compound space. During an active Moloch counterattack, collection waits until settlement.

The largest cavern harvest records producer output deposited with one collection. Supplies withdrawn from Moloch reward reserves do not count. The server starts recording these deposits with 0.13. Source quest IUC5 tests Balthazar discovery and gives two Valgos. IUC10 tests the same discovery and gives 20 Balthazar. Each has its own collection receipt. The Descent achievements cover the first stage and stages 201 through 213. Additional badges recognize Under Hall level five and ten completed cavern objectives.

**Neighbors** shows how many of your mystery sacks other keepers have fully collected. The three milestones are 5, 25 and 50. Previously delivered gifts count if the database contains their completed receipts. Send a personal invitation link to someone who can reach your server. Registering a new keeper through that link advances the inviter’s 1, 5 and 10 registration goals. Their awards are 25, 45 and 65 Shiny respectively. A link containing `localhost` points to the recipient’s own computer. For a networked game, open the game at its reachable server address before copying the link.

Completing the General Store grants its original 1,000 Shiny and a scaled resource award. Building the Radio Tower opens its original resource award. Social registrations and the store award credit the keeper’s existing Shiny balance. Founding or joining an alliance grants a permanent membership badge. Three Map Room 2 badges recognize taking over a wild outpost, taking over five rival keeper outposts, and buying and applying a starter kit. The takeover and kit badges record completed actions from 0.13 onward. The Town Hall badges now run through levels 8 and 10. Two construction badges count 200 finished Blocks and eight finished Heavy Traps across the home and regional outposts. Visiting a field yard collects its completed construction receipts. Kit facilities count when their builds finish. Moving, repairing or upgrading a structure earns no new construction receipt. The supplied achievement table also contains one disabled Huge Rage objective and a blocked placeholder.

The social rule and reward definitions come from `QUESTS.as`. Badge conditions follow `ACHIEVEMENTS.as`, `BASE.as` and `popup_prefab.as`. Overworld and Inferno records in that source are single-completion objectives. Local player registration replaces Facebook referral attribution. Scheduled events retain their own event and prize ledgers.
