# Inferno Emergence

Open **Yard events → Campaigns and weekly hunts → Inferno Emergence** in your home yard. The closed cavern also has a link. An operational level 5 Town Hall and more than 10% combined yard health let you investigate the first earthquake. Previously started events keep their place. The optional level 2 cavern entrance still accepts its original supply payment.

Investigating grows the portal immediately. You can close the story, come back later and engage Moloch’s minions at the same stage. Your towers, garrisons and defending champion face enemies from the western rift. Reloading resumes the same battle. Sounding the siren ends the attack. A ranked invasion takes precedence over the event and starts with the health your defenders retained. The home **Repair Base** button restores damage instantly and for free.

The next earthquake becomes available 12 real hours after the previous one began. The five quakes reveal the Skull of Doom whether Moloch’s attack is repelled or ends in defeat. Resolving the fifth defense opens free cavern entry. The Magma Tower and Quake Tower become available for ordinary paid construction in the home yard. Existing cavern facilities, campaign clears, and earlier paid unlocks carry forward.

The source classes INFERNO_EMERGENCE_EVENT, INFERNOPORTAL and INFERNO_PORTAL_ATTACK supply the interval, portal order, gates, mixed rounding and pack composition. WMATTACK supplies the bearing and XP-based strength. The local server projects the approach onto the playable grid and uses the existing combat simulator. The event runs on demand in each player’s yard. Its five illustrations combine the original two release paintings with three new intermediate paintings. The Moloch portrait, final cavern scene and quake sound were created for this remake.
