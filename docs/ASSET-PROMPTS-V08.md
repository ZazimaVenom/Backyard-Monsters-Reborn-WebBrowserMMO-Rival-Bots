# v0.8 Yard Planner and construction-worker artwork

Four new assets were generated one at a time with the built-in image generator. Two further targeted refinements corrected hammer-hand continuity in the worker work sheet. Accepted generated PNG bytes were copied unchanged into `public/assets/art/`; no resizing, recoloring, cropping, optimization or other programmatic image editing was applied.

## Identity and rendering

The source references are original building ID 10 (`server/public/assets/buildingbuttons/10.jpg` and `buildingthumbs/10.png`) and the blue worker/yellow-hat sprite (`server/public/assets/monsters/worker.png`) in the supplied archive. The new Planner retains the original round stone/wood hut, dark conical roof and attached drafting board. The worker retains the small blue blob body, white eyes and yellow hard hat; a hammer and tool belt clarify its construction action in the HD treatment. Existing `maproom.png` and `bolt.png` supplied rendering-style references. These are new smooth painted illustrations, not restored Flash sprites.

| Asset | Purpose | Geometry | Facing |
|---|---|---|---|
| `planner.png` | Yard Planner building | 1,254 × 1,254 RGBA cutout | Isometric front/right |
| `worker.png` | Worker idle/UI cutout | 1,254 × 1,254 RGBA cutout | Right |
| `worker-walk.png` | Worker travel loop | Four poses; 2 × 2, 627 × 627 cells | Right |
| `worker-work.png` | Construction loop | Four poses; 2 × 2, 627 × 627 cells | Right |

Both sheets use row-major order and fixed union cell bounds at runtime. Walk: contact, passing, opposite swing, recovery. Work: prepare, raised hammer, downstroke, recovery. The near arm on the left side of the image holds the hammer consistently in the accepted work sheet. The downstroke turns the worker's gaze toward its hammer. These are one-view loops; they do not claim full original directional, hurt/death or Inferno-worker parity.
